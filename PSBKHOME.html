Philippine Superbike Championship

The Premier Motorcycle Racing Series in the Philippines

Championship Classes

Underbone Championship

The Underbone Championship features small-displacement, semi-automatic motorcycles popular across Southeast Asia. Races are known for close competition, intense drafting, and skillful cornering, making it a proving ground for young and veteran riders alike.

Scooter Championship

Scooter Championship races highlight automatic transmission scooters, typically 125cc to 180cc. Riders compete in fast-paced sprints, demonstrating agility and control on machines designed for both city commuting and racing circuits.

Superbike Championship

The Superbike Championship is the premier class, featuring high-performance 600cc to 1000cc production-based motorcycles. Top Filipino riders and teams battle at high speeds, showcasing advanced riding skills, technical setups, and thrilling overtakes on the country’s best circuits.
